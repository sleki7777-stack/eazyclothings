# EAZY CLOTHING EXQUISITES

Premium men's fashion house frontend.

**CRAFTED IN LAGOS. DESIGNED FOR EVERYWHERE.**

This repository is intentionally frontend-first and commerce-backend agnostic. The architecture is prepared for future Shopify commerce, EAZY Digital Atelier, EAZY Wardrobe, Runway, textile inventory, production/QC, Master Seal provenance and AI-assisted EAZY Intelligence.

## Core launch scope
- Men's tailoring and African tailoring
- Native / Senator and contemporary native
- Shirting and knitwear
- Outerwear
- Street / relaxed
- Resort
- Denim
- Active / swim
- Lounge / night
- Headwear

Footwear, bags, belts, watches and eyewear are intentionally outside the core EAZY launch experience and reserved for the future commerce layer.

## Engineering principles
- No fake scarcity
- No copied designer garments or protected assets
- AI proposes; EAZY decides
- Real textile and production constraints remain the source of truth
- Shopify is an eventual commerce backend, not the identity of the House
