# EAZY PRODUCT INTEGRITY LAW

## Non-negotiable

**What the customer sees and chooses is exactly what the customer gets — 100%.**

This applies to:

- product identity
- colour
- finish
- material representation
- variant
- price
- image
- source
- provenance
- availability
- collection placement

## Real product images

A purchasable product must use an image tied to the actual source/product.

Never:

- recolour one product image to fake another variant
- generate a product image and pass it off as inventory
- substitute a similar product
- use an editorial image as if it were the purchasable item
- reuse one image for materially different variants
- hide an unverified source behind polished presentation

## Variant law

If a supplier has Gold, Silver, White Silver, Diamond or other materially different variants, each variant must have:

- its own identity
- its own supplier price
- its own EAZY retail price
- its own source evidence
- its own exact image
- verified image rights
- exact-image-match evidence
- availability state
- Shopify variant identity

The customer selects the exact variant.

The selected variant controls the displayed image and displayed price.

## Publication gate

A product is not purchase-ready unless the required approval, source/material/origin/provenance/authenticity evidence, commercial terms, imagery and image-rights checks pass.

When explicit variants exist, every variant must pass its own verification requirements.

## Transparency

The product record should expose, where verified:

- material
- origin
- quality check
- provenance
- approval status
- relevant source information

Missing evidence means pending — not invented.

## Collection imagery

Collection heroes must be composed from actual verified products assigned to that collection.

If no verified products exist:

**COLLECTION IMAGE PENDING / VERIFIED PRODUCTS REQUIRED**

## Design studies

Design studies may exist separately from commerce inventory.

They must be clearly marked:

**DESIGN STUDY — NOT A PRODUCT**

Editorial/Unsplash imagery must never silently become purchasable inventory.

## Customer satisfaction

EAZY should operate toward an exceptional customer-satisfaction standard with rapid escalation and resolution. Do not represent customer satisfaction as a mathematical guarantee that no business can honestly make.
