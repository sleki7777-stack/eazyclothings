# EAZY SOURCING AND CATALOGUE RECORD

## Sourcing philosophy

EAZY is a curated house. A supplier being discoverable does not make a product approved.

Each product must be investigated for:

- actual source
- product identity
- material
- origin
- provenance
- authenticity
- commercial terms
- image rights
- exact source-image match
- availability
- quality
- cultural appropriateness where applicable

## Collection rule

The store should not be filled merely to reach a numerical target.

Best products first.

If a category lacks verified inventory, show an honest pending state rather than fabricating a collection hero or product card.

## Exact-image principle

Supplier variants with materially different appearance must retain their own exact source imagery. No artificial recolouring or substituted imagery.
