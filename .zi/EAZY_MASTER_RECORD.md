# EAZY MASTER RECORD

## 1. House vision

EAZY is being built as a premium fashion ecosystem rather than a generic catalogue.

**EAZY dresses the man. SLEEK EAZY completes him.**

The system should allow a customer to discover, select, design/compose, verify and purchase with confidence.

## 2. EAZY Clothing Exquisites

EAZY Clothing Exquisites covers clothing and complete looks.

Priority men's lanes include:

- Senator / native
- Agbada
- Kaftan
- African traditional wear by cultural lane
- Igbo
- Yoruba / Owo / Oyo
- Benin
- Ghanaian and other African heritage lanes where verified
- chiefs / big men / executive traditional
- traditional wedding groom
- traditional wedding guest
- groom and special-event looks
- birthday / photoshoot looks
- authority/status looks
- premium finishing pieces that complete a garment

The house should curate the **best**, not simply accumulate hundreds of mediocre products.

## 3. SLEEK EAZY

SLEEK EAZY is the accessories house.

Primary categories:

- Watches
- Jewellery
- Eyewear
- Leather
- Footwear
- Ceremony
- Fragrance & Grooming
- After Dark
- Resort
- Objects
- Gifts

SLEEK EAZY is intended for Shopify-backed commerce of accessories and completion pieces, not the current clothing/fabric store.

## 4. SLEEK EAZY pricing architecture

- Below House Range: below ₦20,000
- CORE: ₦20,000–₦200,000
- SIGNATURE: ₦200,000–₦300,000
- PREMIUM: ₦300,000–₦600,000
- LIMITED EDITION: ₦600,000+

Directional catalogue target:

- 350 Core
- 75 Signature
- 20 Premium
- 10 Limited
- 455 total directional target

The target is directional, not permission to fill the store with weak products.

## 6. Collections

Every commerce collection requires:

1. A collection image.
2. A truthful collection summary.
3. Image composition made from actual verified products inside that collection.
4. No generic editorial image presented as inventory.
5. A pending state when verified products do not yet exist.

## 7. Customer psychology / offers

The house may use strong first-purchase incentives, including the previously discussed ₦15k–₦25k first-purchase qualification concept tied to a meaningful first order range, but offers must be implemented deliberately and economically rather than as blanket discounts.

Cross-store purchases should be capable of receiving a coordinated EAZY benefit when the business rules are finalized.

## 8. House standard

Premium curation, evidence, provenance, transparency, exact imagery, variant accuracy, supplier truth and customer confidence are foundational requirements.

The goal is not merely to sell an object.

The goal is to make the customer trust that the EAZY object, image, description, variant and delivered item are the same decision.
